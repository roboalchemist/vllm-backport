Measured and reproduced: the TP all-reduce over the Gen2 x4 fabric is the prefill ceiling. Removing it (TP1×PP6) lifts prefill from ~2.5k to 5.6k tok/s @32k and 7.2k @128k — matching Schaka's 5,078 t/s claim. The pipeline hop is far cheaper than the all-reduce.

  • Brought up and validated the no-all-reduce layout, then measured the full matrix.

The tradeoff: removing the all-reduce triples prefill but costs decode — PP6 adds 5 pipeline-hop latencies per token and splits the KV less, so c1 drops 43→22 and 512k c64 drops 130→75. The relay fix is correct only for TP1×PP6; TP2×PP4 and TP1×PP8 still produce garbage (needle 0/3), because the relay does not yet handle a TP split of the relayed caches.

3.Decision

  • Serve TP4×PP2 — best for the 512k aggregate-decode target and validated 12/12 long-context.
  • TP1×PP6 stays available as the prefill-optimized profile (2.5–3× prefill, correct) for prefill-heavy use.
  • 500 tok/s @512k remains unmet by either; the paths are a correct TP-split relay and/or a faster attention kernel.
