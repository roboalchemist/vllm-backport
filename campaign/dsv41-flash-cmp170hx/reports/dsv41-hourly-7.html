1.Correction: depth does raise per-token cost

An earlier note claimed "per-stream TPOT is flat with depth" — that used the harness's aggregate tpot_ms (wall / total tokens). True per-stream TPOT = wall × concurrency / completion_tokens and it grows with depth.
Contextc32 aggregate (tok/s)per-stream TPOTper-stream (tok/s)
4k302.8106 ms9.5
32k305.4105 ms9.5
128k214.0150 ms6.7
512k108.8294 ms3.4
512k (c64)126.9504 ms2.0

So the 512k aggregate is ≈ concurrency / per-stream TPOT, and per-stream TPOT rises ~5× from 4k to 512k. Reaching 500 tok/s @512k needs ~5× lower per-token cost at depth or ~4× more concurrency headroom.
