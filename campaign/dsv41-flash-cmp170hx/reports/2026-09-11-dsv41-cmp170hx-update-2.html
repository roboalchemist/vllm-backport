5.Our campaign status — now running Schaka's recipe

Our own engine (dsv41-feat @ e47aa780) boots and serves but emits incoherent text; the failure localises to a ~150× shrink in the attention output projection. Rather than keep debugging, we pivoted to the one proven path:

  • Pulled base lazymio/vllm-backport:v0.12.0-sm80; applied upstream PR #56201 + Schaka's fixups.py + ampere/ shims + PP-KV relay → 92 changed files.
  • Built localhost/vllm-backport-v41:sm80 as a Python overlay (no native rebuild) — matching his published kit.
  • Launched dsv41-schaka on :8090: TP4×PP2, TRITON_MLA_SPARSE_DSV41, fp8_ds_mla, block-128, DSpark k=5, max-model-len 1,048,576, VLLM_PP_LAYER_PARTITION=20,20.
  • Status at write time: loading weights (GPUs resident ~38 GiB each; DeepseekV41ForCausalLM, max len 1048576).
Why his recipe matters: it closes exactly the SM80 gaps we hit — the MXFP8 block-scale layout ([N/32,K/32]→[N,K/32]), routing wo_a to the emulation kernel (Marlin repacks 4 fp8→int32), replacing the V4.0 9-arg qnorm op (dropping V4.1's apply_q_norm normalises Q twice → wrong output, no error), the CuteDSL SM gate, the compressor cache groups, and a PP relay that makes the layers-20–39 KV-sharing group splittable.
